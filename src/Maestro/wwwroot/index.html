    Settings

    Spotify

    Maestro plays and searches through your Spotify desktop app (SpotX works great), so a free account is fine and no keys are needed. Maestro opens Spotify in the background by itself — you never have to open it.

    YouTube

    Search works without a key. A YouTube Data API key is optional and just makes search and big playlist imports more reliable.

    SoundCloud

    Public tracks play inside Maestro. No account or API key needed. SoundCloud Go+ exclusives and tracks that block embedding may not play.
