Why Use Maestro as Your Music Aggregator?

Unified multi-service search

Search Spotify, YouTube, and SoundCloud simultaneously side-by-side with a single query. Filter or view all results at once.

Mixed multi-source queues

Drag, drop, and combine Spotify tracks, YouTube videos, and SoundCloud sets into one playlist. Maestro switches sources automatically as songs end.

Automatic volume normalization

Dynamic audio leveling balances volume between Spotify, YouTube, and SoundCloud so you never have to adjust your volume between tracks.

No Premium or API keys needed

Spotify plays natively through your local Spotify desktop app (free accounts fully supported). YouTube and SoundCloud stream without API keys.

Universal link paste

Paste any URL from Spotify, YouTube, or SoundCloud into Maestro to instantly play, enqueue, or save up to 100 tracks to your personal library.

Privacy-first desktop architecture

Runs 100% locally on your Windows machine with an embedded WebView2 UI and in-process C# API. Your listening history stays on your computer.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

What is a music streaming aggregator app?

A music streaming aggregator app connects multiple streaming services into a single interface. Instead of opening separate apps for Spotify, YouTube, and SoundCloud, an aggregator lets you search, queue, and play audio across all of them in one place.

What is Maestro?

Maestro is a universal music streaming aggregator app for Windows, free for personal use. It unifies Spotify, YouTube, and SoundCloud into a single desktop player with concurrent search and a shared queue.

Do I need Spotify Premium to use Maestro?

No. Maestro works with standard free Spotify accounts by connecting to your local Spotify desktop app via Chrome DevTools Protocol debugging. It also plays YouTube and SoundCloud with no subscriptions or API keys.

Can I mix Spotify, YouTube, and SoundCloud in the same queue or playlist?

Yes. Maestro is built specifically to allow seamless mixing of tracks from Spotify, YouTube, and SoundCloud. As each track finishes, Maestro automatically transitions to the next service.

How does Maestro handle volume differences between services?

Maestro includes real-time audio volume normalization, analyzing loudness on YouTube and SoundCloud tracks and evening them out to match Spotify's playback level.

What platforms does Maestro support?

Maestro is built for Windows 10 and Windows 11 (64-bit). Support for macOS and Linux is not available yet.

Is Maestro free?

Yes. Maestro is free for individual, personal use, and its complete source code is available on GitHub under the Business Source License 1.1. Production use by a business requires a separate license, and every release converts to GPL four years after its release.
